Nutrition Tips
Evidence-based strategies to optimise your diet and improve your health.
🥦 Vegetables

Fill Half Your Plate with Veggies

Non-starchy vegetables are nutrient-dense and low in calories. Aim for a variety of colours to maximise micronutrient intake.

💧 Hydration

Drink Water Before Every Meal

Drinking 500ml of water 30 minutes before meals can reduce calorie intake by up to 13% and boost metabolism temporarily.

🏋️ Protein

Prioritise Protein at Breakfast

A high-protein breakfast reduces hunger hormones and cravings throughout the day, supporting fat loss and muscle maintenance.

🌙 Timing

Avoid Eating 2–3 Hours Before Bed

Late-night eating can disrupt sleep quality and metabolism. If hungry, opt for a small protein-rich snack like Greek yogurt.

🍎 Fibre

Aim for 25–35g of Fibre Daily

Dietary fibre improves gut health, stabilises blood sugar, and keeps you full longer. Include legumes, whole grains, and fruit.

🧠 Mindful Eating

Eat Slowly and Without Distraction

It takes 20 minutes for your brain to register fullness. Eating mindfully helps prevent overeating and improves digestion.
